# Mythroot Hearth Continental Dossier v0.1

**Status:** Source-preserved working dossier.

Hearth is the mostly intact great continent of Aruun. Its identity is connected abundance: broad ecological continuity, large river systems, long migration corridors, and diverse climates linked across a single continental mass.

This document remains a source dossier rather than a replacement for the World Bible. Detailed geography, hydrology, climate boundaries, settlement placement, and regional culture remain subject to the domain documents and working models.
