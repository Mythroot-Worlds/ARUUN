# MYTHROOT — LOST CONTINENTAL DOSSIER
## Geography & Climate Reference — v0.1

**Purpose:** Standalone reference for mapmaking and future regional development.

## Geographic identity
Lost is one enormous continuous continent separated from the main continental cluster by an immense ocean. Working isolation: roughly 700 million–1 billion years.

Its geology continued evolving throughout isolation.

## Major geographic structures
- enormous interior
- independent mountain systems
- ancient rounded ranges
- younger mountain systems
- plateaus
- large interior basins
- ancient/recurring inland lakes and wetlands
- long ocean-facing margins
- local volcanic/active regions

## Climate
Lost is not climatically magical. Its climate follows latitude, circulation, currents, elevation, mountains, and continentality.

### Warm/moist coasts
Dense forests, wetlands, coastal grasslands, large deltas.

### Cooler/drier current coasts
Cooler grasslands, dry woodland, scrub, seasonally arid regions.

### Interior
Seasonal woodland, enormous grasslands, dry forests, semi-arid basins, localized deserts.

### Mountains
Wet windward slopes, dry rain shadows, highlands, cool valleys, isolated ecological regions.

### Interior basins
Potential enormous inland lakes, wetlands, seasonal lakes, saline basins, and dry-basin ecosystems.

## Ecological identity
**Deep-time independence.** Its eventual biological difference comes primarily from hundreds of millions of years of independent evolution, not alien climate physics.

## Artist's Map Brief
Show one enormous continuous landmass, huge interior distances, multiple independent mountain systems, ancient and younger ranges, broad plateaus, major basins, large rivers, potential inland seas/lakes, and varied coastal environments.
