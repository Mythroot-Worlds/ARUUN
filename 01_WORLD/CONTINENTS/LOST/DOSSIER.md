# Mythroot Lost Continental Dossier v0.1

**Status:** Source-preserved working dossier.

Lost is an enormous continuous continent isolated from the other continental systems by an immense ocean. Its long isolation is a major geological and evolutionary constraint and should produce a distinctive ecological history.

The working isolation timescale is roughly 700 million–1 billion years. This is a geological/evolutionary constraint, not a claim that Aruunites have been independently evolving there for that entire interval.
