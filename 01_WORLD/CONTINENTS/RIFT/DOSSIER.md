# Mythroot Rift Continental Dossier v0.1

**Status:** Source-preserved working dossier.

Rift is a great continental system actively splitting into two major halves. Continental crust is thinning; faults, basins, volcanism, lakes, wetlands, and strong environmental gradients characterize the system.

The rift remains a geological process rather than a static fantasy feature. Its geography should continue to evolve through tectonic reasoning.
