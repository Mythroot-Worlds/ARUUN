# Mythroot Shattered Continental Dossier v0.1

**Status:** Source-preserved working dossier.

Shattered consists of enormous continental fragments separated by major seas. Its defining ecological and geographic pressure is fragmentation: neighboring fragments can diverge in climate, ecology, and population history while remaining part of the same continental system.

The fragments result from overlapping rifts, transform/oblique motion, rotation, and accretion rather than arbitrary fragmentation.
