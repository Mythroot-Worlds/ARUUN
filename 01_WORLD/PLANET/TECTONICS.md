# Mythroot Prehistoric World — Tectonic Model v0.2

## Status

**Working scientific model — not locked canon.**

This revision is the actual tectonic reasoning pass. Earlier visual concepts should be treated only as visualizations of previous conceptual models, not as authoritative geography.

## Scientific constraints used

The model uses real plate-tectonic mechanisms:

- divergent boundaries create new oceanic crust;
- convergent oceanic margins can subduct, producing trenches, earthquakes, volcanic arcs, and mountain building;
- transform boundaries accommodate horizontal motion and can offset otherwise continuous systems;
- continental rifts create fault-bounded basins and may either fail or mature into new ocean basins;
- oblique convergence can produce rotation and strike-slip displacement;
- continental fragments and volcanic arcs can accrete to larger continental margins.

## Creative constraints

The present world must contain:

1. A mostly intact great continent.
2. A fragmented great continental system consisting of several enormous pieces.
3. A great continental system currently splitting into two major halves.
4. A huge, continuous Lost Continent separated from the others by an enormous ocean.
5. The three main continental systems grouped in one broad region.
6. The Lost Continent isolated for roughly 700 million–1 billion years as a working timescale.
7. A world that feels prehistoric rather than fantastical: geology should look old, active, and natural.
8. No need for an Earth-identical continental arrangement.

## Actual tectonic architecture

### Plate H — Hearth Plate
Carries most of the stable continent. Motion: slow northwestward drift relative to the main oceanic system.

### Plate S — Shatter-West Plate
Carries western/central fragments of the Shattered Continent. Motion: generally southwestward with a rotational component.

### Plate SE — Shatter-East Plate
Carries eastern fragments of the Shattered Continent. Motion: generally southeastward relative to Shatter-West, with slow rotation.

### Rift Crown and Rift South Plates
Together represent the actively separating Rift system. Continental crust thins, faults and basins form, volcanism increases, and full ocean formation has not yet occurred.

### Oceanic Ring Plate
Provides the surrounding oceanic framework and major subduction/transform boundaries.

### Lost Plate
Carries the isolated Lost Continent and represents its long-term independent tectonic history.

## Geological identity

Aruun should feel old, tectonically active, and naturally evolved. Continental positions and landforms are consequences of geological mechanisms rather than arbitrary fantasy map shapes.
