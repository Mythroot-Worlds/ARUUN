# MYTHROOT WORLDS — FLORA CREATION MATRIX v0.1

## Purpose

Use this matrix to rapidly create plausible canonical plants from environmental and ecological requirements.

Core formula:

> **CONTINENT → CLIMATE → FUNCTION → EVOLUTIONARY POSSIBILITIES → PLANT**

# 12. CONTINENTAL BIASES

## Hearth
Favor:
- broad-ranging flora;
- connected plant populations;
- large continuous vegetation zones;
- widespread generalists;
- extensive grasslands, forests, wetlands, and river systems.

## Shattered
Favor:
- regional variants;
- island/fragment endemics;
- coastal plants;
- salt tolerance;
- dispersal across water;
- rapid divergence;
- local specialization.

## Rift
Favor:
- wet/dry transition specialists;
- lake and river plants;
- highland flora;
- volcanic pioneers;
- disturbance-adapted plants;
- sharp ecological zonation.

## Lost
Favor:
- deep-time independent lineages;
- unusual plant architectures;
- convergent solutions to familiar functions;
- ancient lineages;
- highly specialized communities;
- unusual but functional growth strategies.

Lost should be evolutionarily different, not randomly fantastical.

# 13. PLANT SCALE

The project's broad vegetation aesthetic favors **thick, robust growth over absurd vertical height**.

---

# 15. RAPID-BUILD RULE

Do not attempt to invent every plant.

Build:
1. foundational producers;
2. major herbivore food sources;
3. major browse resources;
4. wetland/aquatic vegetation;
5. soil/ground-cover plants;
6. disturbance/pioneer plants;
7. region-defining plants;
8. a limited number of distinctive signature plants.

Stop when the ecosystem has enough functional coverage to support the animal library and make each region visually/ecologically distinct.

---

# 16. FINAL TEST

Before accepting a plant:

**Does it have a job?**

**Does the climate explain it?**

**Does the continent affect its distribution or divergence?**

**Does its anatomy/physiology solve a real problem?**

**Does it have a tradeoff?**

**Does it interact with animals or other plants?**

**Does it make the ecosystem more believable?**

**Is it actually different enough to deserve its own canonical entry?**

If not, revise, merge, or leave it as an implied background organism.
