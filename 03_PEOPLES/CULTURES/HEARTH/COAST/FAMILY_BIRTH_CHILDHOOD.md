# HEARTH PEOPLES — COAST
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON

- Birth is a community event; biological parentage remains recognized.
- Infants live within the communal environment and learn by observing everyone.
- Children rotate through fishing, gathering, hunting, land reading, guarding, weaving, tools, food processing, and preservation.
- Everyone learns broad survival skills rather than inheriting a narrow role from parents.
- Adolescents increasingly participate fully in communal work cycles.
- Adulthood means being able to care for oneself and contribute reliably to the community.

**Principle:** Become broadly capable and dependable.
