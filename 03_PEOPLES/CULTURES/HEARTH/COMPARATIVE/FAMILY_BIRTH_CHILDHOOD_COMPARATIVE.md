# HEARTH — COMPARATIVE REFERENCE
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON REFERENCE SHEET

This sheet compares the six regional developmental systems. The regional files under each People are the authoritative cultural entries; this document exists as a cross-cultural reference for creators and users who want to utilize Hearth to maximum depth.

| People | Developmental emphasis |
|---|---|
| Plains | Carry your share |
| Mountains | Master your lineage's knowledge |
| River | Read the water and know the network |
| Wetlands | Become useful to your group and village |
| Desert | Learn knowledge from elders, then prove it in practice |
| Coast | Become broadly capable and dependable |

These systems are distinct responses to the same survival problem: turning children into adults capable of keeping their communities alive.
