# HEARTH CULTURAL MATRIX — RELIGION, BELIEF, RITUAL & SPIRITUAL LIFE

**Status:** ANALYTICAL REFERENCE — NOT CANON
**Purpose:** Source-derived boundary map for Hearth spirituality and belief.
**Authority:** Regional cultural documents remain authoritative. This matrix does not invent religions, reconcile gaps, or promote historical seeds into canon.

## 1. CURRENT RESULT

This is currently the least-developed major Hearth cultural layer. The Hearth Cultural Audit Checklist marks **Belief / Worldview as GAP for all six regional peoples**. Missing material is explicitly intended to be developed from geography, ecology, history, and existing canon rather than guessed.

Therefore this document is intentionally a **gap map**, not a religion generator.

## 2. WORLD-LEVEL MATERIAL THAT IS ESTABLISHED

The broader Aruun setting establishes:

- Magic is real but very low-grade.
- Magic is not a game system.
- There are no mana pools, levels, spell lists, numerical effects, or quantified healing.
- Rituals, chants, gestures, preparations, herbs, pastes, smoke, blood, and similar practices can have genuine effects.
- Magic can restore, stabilize, encourage, or improve the chance of recovery.
- Magic cannot guarantee an outcome.
- Magic can influence an outcome but cannot dictate it.
- Material practices may have ordinary biological/environmental explanations while a small spiritual presence also interacts with the natural world.
- The exact metaphysical mechanism remains open.

Historical concept material also contains possible spiritual practices such as healing, spiritual journeys, dream-walking, interpreting spirits, unusual medicines, ritual practice, animal relationships, supernatural knowledge, and rare alterations of body or mind. Those are **design seeds**, not automatically Hearth canon.

## 4. SAFE SHARED FOUNDATION

Hearth exists inside a world where:

**nature → ecology → survival → culture**

is a major causal chain.

Spirituality is part of the world design foundation, but the six Hearth peoples have not yet been given final expressions of that relationship.

Magic does **not** automatically equal religion. A ritual can have a magical effect without establishing gods, churches, priesthoods, or a universal cosmology. Conversely, a spiritual practice can be culturally meaningful without being magically effective.

## 6. IMPORTANT DESIGN DISTINCTION

Do not automatically create six unrelated religions merely because there are six peoples.

Existing Hearth relationships leave open at least two coherent models:

1. **Shared ancient traditions + regional interpretations + local ritual variation**
2. **Distinct regional spiritual systems with overlapping historical roots**

The repository does not yet select between them.

## 7. DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

1. **Low magic:** spirituality exists alongside subtle, consequence-preserving magic.
2. **No automatic high fantasy:** do not introduce conventional gods, mana economies, spellcasting institutions, or miracle systems unless deliberately chosen.
3. **Ecology matters:** belief should relate to actual environments.
4. **Survival matters:** spirituality should fit the setting's knowledge/cooperation/adaptation foundation.
5. **Knowledge can overlap with spirituality:** healer, tracker, elder, Lifekeeper, or specialist does not automatically mean priest.
6. **Ritual does not guarantee magic.**
7. **Historical seed material is not regional canon.**
