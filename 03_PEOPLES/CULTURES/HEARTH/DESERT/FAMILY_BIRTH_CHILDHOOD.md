# HEARTH PEOPLES — DESERT
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON

- Birth occurs within the oasis community with experienced support.
- Infants remain close to family and learn early that water must not be wasted.
- Education deliberately has two stages: **theory and practice**.
- Earlier in the day, elders teach plants, animals, weather, terrain, water conservation, tracks, resources, survival rules, stories, and accumulated knowledge.
- Later, experienced adults take children into the environment and demonstrate those lessons.
- Children rotate through elders, gatherers, hunters, Water Watchers, animal handlers, Lifekeepers, and other experienced adults rather than learning only from parents.
- Parents remain deeply involved but are not the child's entire educational system.
- Adolescents begin supervised travel between nearby settlements; exceptional candidates may later attempt the Lifekeeper Trials.
- Ordinary adulthood is demonstrated by responsible handling of scarce resources, especially water.

**Principle:** The elders teach the knowledge. The experienced show the practice. The child learns by doing.
