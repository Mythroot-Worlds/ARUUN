# MYTHROOT WORLDS — HEARTH PEOPLES
## Mountain Family, Partnership & Specialist-Lineage Update v0.1
**Status:** WORKING UPDATE — NOT CANON

### Mountain settlement scale
Mountain settlements are now modeled at **50–70 people**. A 60-person midpoint is **not canon** and is not a fixed target.

### Specialist-lineage structure
The Mountain specialist system remains lineage/house based, but smaller settlements require broader houses. A house will maintain **multiple related competencies** rather than one narrow occupation.

The exact number of houses, their names, and their competency bundles are **OPEN**. They will be determined later by asking what arrangement allows a 50–70-person settlement to cover all essential functions while preserving deep specialist knowledge.

### Partnership
The existing same-house/same-specialization marriage system remains:
- partners are generally selected within the same specialist house;
- the Mountain Conclave coordinates inter-community exchanges;
- relevant house heads compare specialist populations;
- the couple remains with whichever village needs that house's people more.

### Conclave
The annual Mountain Conclave continues to serve marriage exchange, specialist population balancing, knowledge exchange, trade, and regional coordination.

### Explicitly undecided
No fixed number of houses, three-house structure, six-specialization list, house names, or population quotas are established.
