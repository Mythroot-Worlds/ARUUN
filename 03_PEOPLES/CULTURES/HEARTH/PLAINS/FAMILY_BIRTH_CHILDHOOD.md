# HEARTH PEOPLES — PLAINS
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON

- Birth is supported by experienced women; anchor communities or slower groups provide stability when needed.
- Infants are carried while adults work and learn through constant observation.
- Children gradually copy useful adult tasks; aptitude is observed, but community need can override preference.
- Adulthood is demonstrated by reliable contribution to survival.

**Principle:** Carry your share.
