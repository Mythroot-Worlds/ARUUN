# HEARTH PEOPLES — RIVER
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON

- Birth is supported by the extended household and community.
- Infants are gradually and safely introduced to water.
- Children learn fishing, gathering, swimming, currents, plants, animals, boats/rafts, and weather.
- Adolescents accompany experienced adults on supervised journeys to other river settlements.
- Adulthood means being able to travel the river safely enough to be useful.

**Principle:** Read the water and know the network.
