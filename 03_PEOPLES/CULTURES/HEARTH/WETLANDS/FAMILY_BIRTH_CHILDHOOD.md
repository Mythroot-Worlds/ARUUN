# HEARTH PEOPLES — WETLANDS
## Birth, Childhood & Coming-of-Age

**Status:** CANON

- Birth is primarily supported by the 12–15-person functional group.
- Infants are cared for by many adults within the group.
- Children rotate through gathering, fishing, hunting, weaving, food processing, scouting, and terrain/water awareness.
- Adolescents can spend seasons with other groups to discover aptitude and spread knowledge.
- Adulthood means being trusted to carry an independent portion of the group's survival burden.

**Principle:** Become useful to your group and village.
