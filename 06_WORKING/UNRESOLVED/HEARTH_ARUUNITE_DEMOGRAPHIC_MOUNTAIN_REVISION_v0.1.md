# HEARTH — ARUUNITE DEMOGRAPHIC WORKING MODEL
## Mountain Settlement Revision v0.1
**Status:** WORKING MODEL — NOT CANON

### Revised Mountain settlement size
**50–70 people per settlement.**

The previously used 60-person midpoint is **not canon** and should not be treated as a fixed target.

### Consequence for specialization
Smaller Mountain settlements cannot safely rely on many narrowly specialized lineages. The specialist system therefore consolidates related competencies into broader houses while retaining deep lineage-based expertise.

The exact house count and skill bundles remain open.

Future simulations should use the full 50–70 range rather than assuming 60.

Other demographic values remain provisional pending further simulation.
