# MYTHROOT WORLDS — CHANGELOG ADDITION
## Hearth / Desert Peoples — Lifekeepers

### Locked decisions

1. Desert society is a mixed network of oasis communities and mobile travelers.
2. Traveling hunters are a major connective institution between oases.
3. These hunters are provisionally called **Lifekeepers**.
4. Lifekeepers are recruited from across the Desert population rather than restricted to one family or oasis.
5. Recruitment is based on demonstrated potential, physical competence, endurance, judgment, teamwork, environmental awareness, self-control, and willingness to learn.
6. Experienced Lifekeepers train prospective hunters.
7. Lifekeepers rotate among multiple oasis communities and distribute meat, environmental information, route knowledge, news, and warnings.
8. Oasis communities reciprocate with water, food, shelter, repairs, equipment, animal support, and recovery space.
9. Lifekeepers are respected as people who help keep the population alive, but they are not a ruling class.
10. A formal **Lifekeeper Trials** tradition is established.
11. The Trials include physical endurance/load-carrying, controlled martial competition, and judgment/survival scenarios.
12. The martial component is the first formal recurring martial contest established for a Hearth people.
13. The Trials also function as a multi-oasis social gathering for training, exchange, storytelling, trade, courtship, games, and celebration.
14. Mountain and Desert isolation are explicitly differentiated:
   - Mountain: isolated communities deliberately reconnect through kinship/marriage.
   - Desert: the desert isolates the population physically, while mobile Lifekeepers actively connect oasis communities.

### Core cultural phrase

> **Of the people, for the people.**

This is a working cultural principle, not necessarily a literal formal motto.
