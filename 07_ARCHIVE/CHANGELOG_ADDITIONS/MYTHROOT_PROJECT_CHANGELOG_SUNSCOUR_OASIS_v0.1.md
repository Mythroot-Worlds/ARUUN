# MYTHROOT WORLDS — CHANGELOG ADDITION
## 2026-08-23 — Hearth / Sunscour Oasis Network

### Decision
Sunscour's desert water system is now a science-derived working geographic layer.

### Added
- Five major perennial oasis systems.
- Six to ten secondary spring/groundwater systems as a regional working target.
- Numerous seasonal and emergency water sites.
- Groundwater recharge from surrounding mountains.
- Alluvial-fan, fault, canyon-mouth, and geological-contact oasis formation logic.
- Saline/playa-dominated basin center rather than a freshwater-centered desert.
- Human concentration around reliable water rather than even distribution.

### Major oasis placements
1. Northwest mountain-front / Stonehearth transition.
2. Northeast Aegir foothill margin.
3. Eastern fault-line corridor.
4. Southern Southward Range foothill margin.
5. Southwestern canyon/fan margin.

### Cultural consequence
The Desert peoples are now established as a **mixed network culture**:
- relatively stationary/semi-stationary oasis communities;
- highly mobile desert travelers.

Neither lifestyle is considered a separate people.
