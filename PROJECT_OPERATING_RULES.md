# MYTHROOT WORLDS — ARUUN PROJECT OPERATING RULES

**Status:** CANON — PROJECT WORKFLOW
**Purpose:** Persistent operating rules for AI-assisted ARUUN worldbuilding and repository development.

## 1. Repository-First Research Rule

Before creating, modifying, answering questions about, or making a substantive decision concerning ARUUN, first search the ARUUN repository for relevant existing information.

The repository is the primary source of truth for established ARUUN worldbuilding.

Relevant searches should include whatever layers are necessary to understand the question, including:

- world geography;
- geology and tectonics;
- climate and atmospheric conditions;
- hydrology and water systems;
- population and demographic models;
- creature/fauna library;
- flora and food/ecology library;
- Peoples and regional cultures;
- technology and material culture;
- settlement and infrastructure models;
- existing canon/decision documents;
- working models and prior analyses;
- other documents that materially constrain the subject.

Do not rely on general knowledge when repository material may already answer the question.

## 2. Understand the System Before Deriving New Material

Do not treat a single document as the entire context for a decision when the repository contains related environmental, demographic, ecological, cultural, or technological layers.

For substantive worldbuilding decisions, reconstruct the relevant chain where applicable:

**world conditions → ecology → resources → organisms → human survival pressures → population → social adaptation → culture → settlement/technology.**

The objective is to derive new ARUUN material from the world that already exists rather than repeatedly rebuilding it from memory or generic Earth assumptions.

## 3. Source Status Labels

When working with repository material, distinguish clearly between:

### CANON
Explicitly established and authoritative unless superseded by a later canon decision.

### WORKING MODEL
A current model being used for development, but not yet formally locked as canon.

### INFERENCE
A conclusion logically derived from established repository information but not itself explicitly established.

### PROPOSAL
New material being suggested for consideration. It is not canon until deliberately accepted.

Never silently convert an inference or proposal into canon.

## 4. Conflicts and Supersession

If repository documents conflict:

1. Identify the conflict.
2. Check dates, versioning, status labels, and later decision documents.
3. Prefer an explicit later canon decision over an earlier working document.
4. Do not silently overwrite conflicting material.
5. If the conflict cannot be resolved from the repository, present it for a decision.

Older documents may preserve development history and should not be treated as current merely because they still exist.

## 5. User Decisions Override Working Assumptions

When the Creative Director explicitly decides or locks a worldbuilding point, treat that decision as the current authority and record it appropriately in the repository when requested or when it is part of a persistent canon change.

Do not repeatedly reopen settled decisions unless new information creates a genuine contradiction or the Creative Director asks to revisit them.

## 6. Scientific Derivation Rule

Aruun uses Earth human history and anthropology as a baseline for what humans can plausibly develop, but Earth history is not a hard technological or cultural ceiling.

Use this hierarchy:

**Earth human precedent → Aruun environmental/evolutionary pressures → scientifically plausible divergence.**

Different planetary conditions may produce human behaviors, technologies, institutions, or cultural patterns that Earth humans did not develop. Conversely, Earth practices should not be assumed to exist on Aruun without environmental, material, or cultural support.

Science constrains plausibility; it does not eliminate creative divergence.

## 7. Check Relevant Libraries Before Inventing Details

If a creature, plant, food source, material, environmental feature, population figure, or other established element might already exist in the repository, search for it before answering or inventing a replacement.

In particular, the Creature Library and Flora/Food Ecology Library should be checked before making assumptions about:

- animal size or behavior;
- reproduction or population density;
- prey/predator relationships;
- food availability;
- plant abundance or seasonality;
- domestication or animal management;
- carrying capacity;
- storage or processing requirements.

## 8. Derive Numbers From the Existing World

When calculating population, family size, settlement size, animal numbers, resource requirements, carrying capacity, or physical footprint, use established variables wherever possible.

Prefer:

**established inputs → explicit assumptions → calculation → sanity check against ecology/culture → working result.**

Do not choose convenient numbers merely to fill a gap when the repository contains enough information to reverse-engineer a better answer.

Clearly identify any remaining assumptions.

## 9. Universal Survival Learning

Do not automatically import modern institutional structures into Aruun.

Aruun is a survival-driven world. Where the established culture indicates experiential learning, assume people learn through observation, participation, practice, and progressively greater responsibility rather than inventing schools, childcare institutions, or specialized facilities without evidence.

This is a general rule, not a prohibition: if a repository-established culture develops a dedicated institution, preserve and use it.

## 10. Regional Repository Architecture

For regional Peoples/cultures, the authoritative development structure is **one regional People per directory and one authoritative file per subject**.

Example:

```text
HEARTH/
├── PLAINS/
│   ├── FAMILY_BIRTH_CHILDHOOD.md
│   ├── FAMILY_PARTNERSHIP.md
│   └── ...
├── MOUNTAINS/
│   ├── FAMILY_BIRTH_CHILDHOOD.md
│   ├── FAMILY_PARTNERSHIP.md
│   └── ...
├── RIVER/
│   └── ...
├── WETLANDS/
│   └── ...
├── DESERT/
│   └── ...
└── COAST/
    └── ...
```

The exact existing repository directory structure should be respected, but the principle is fixed:

> **One People/region → one authoritative regional entry per subject.**

## 11. Batch Creation Does Not Change Architecture

If the Creative Director asks for material for all six regions at once to save time, generate six regional entries using the established regional template.

Batching is a production method, not a reason to create a consolidated canonical document.

Do not place six regional canon entries into one comparative file merely because they were created in one batch.

## 12. Comparative and Creator Reference Sheets

Comparative/reference sheets are a secondary layer for readers, creators, GMs, and other users who want to use ARUUN/Hearth to its maximum depth.

They should be created **after the underlying regional canon is accurate and complete enough to support them**, especially during final packaging.

Comparative sheets should derive from the authoritative regional files rather than becoming competing sources of canon.

## 13. Preserve Existing Canon During Reorganization

When reorganizing files, preserve established wording and decisions unless the task explicitly includes revision.

Separate:

- restructuring;
- canon correction;
- substantive worldbuilding development.

Do not use a file-organization task as an excuse to silently rewrite culture or worldbuilding.

## 14. When Information Is Missing

If repository research does not answer a question, say so.

Then either:

- derive a clearly labeled inference from the available evidence;
- make a clearly labeled proposal;
- or ask the Creative Director for the missing decision.

Do not pretend an unsupported detail was already established.

## 15. Default Working Loop

For substantive ARUUN work:

1. **Search the repository.**
2. **Read the relevant sources.**
3. **Identify established canon and current working models.**
4. **Check related ecological, demographic, geographic, cultural, and technological constraints.**
5. **Identify conflicts or gaps.**
6. **Derive the answer or model from the available evidence.**
7. **Clearly label inference/proposal where applicable.**
8. **Present the reasoning and result for Creative Director review.**
9. **Only then update canon or repository files when directed.**

This operating rule exists to prevent context loss, duplicate invention, accidental contradictions, and batch-production architecture drift while preserving the flexibility and scientific grounding of ARUUN development.
