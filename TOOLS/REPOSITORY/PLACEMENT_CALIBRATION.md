# ARUUN Content Placement Calibration Rules

These rules define what the placement audit is trying to recognize. They are intentionally advisory; they do not move or rewrite canon.

## 1. World artifact
A document describing something that exists in ARUUN: creatures, habitats, geography, cultures, settlements, resources, behaviors, distributions, measurements, or other world facts.

## 2. Construction method
A document whose primary purpose is to explain how a world element was generated or derived: algorithms, formulas, simulations, creation procedures, matrices used as generation logic, or design methodology.

Construction methods belong in the Tools layer and may be referenced by world artifacts.

## 3. World artifact with construction provenance
A world-facing artifact may have been produced using a construction method without becoming a tool itself. A regional map, ecology description, or creature library can remain canon/world-facing while pointing to the method that produced it.

## 4. Creature library rule
The canonical creature library is world-facing. Creature entries describe what exists: identity, habitat, distribution, size, food, behavior, ecology, and other world facts. Creation methodology belongs in a separate construction-method document.

## 5. SunsCour Oasis rule
SunsCour Oasis Network is treated as a world artifact when describing the resulting Desert geography/water network. The climate/hydrology simulation or design method that produced it is construction methodology and should be separate or linked as provenance.

## 6. Archive rule
Archive material is historical source material, not current truth. It may contain useful fragments and competing ideas, but it must not be treated as an authoritative replacement for the World Bible or an authoritative deep-lore document.

## 7. Authority rule
There should be one authoritative current document for a subject at its intended layer. Similar documents are lineage candidates until a creator resolves their relationship. The audit must never silently select a canonical winner.

## 8. Review rule
Placement findings are recommendations. Human review decides KEEP, MERGE, MOVE, LINK, ARCHIVE, or UNRESOLVED.
