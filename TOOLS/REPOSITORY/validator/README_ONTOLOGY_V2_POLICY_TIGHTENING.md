# Ontology v2 policy tightening

This note records the intended boundary exposed by the true-blind benchmark.

## Relationship routing principle

`RELATED` is the default for documents that share a cultural/domain framework but address different subjects or functions.

`VARIANT` requires evidence that the documents represent the **same underlying subject or cultural artifact at different layers, regional expressions, scopes, or versions**.

In particular:

- SAME category + DIFFERENT subject + DIFFERENT context is not sufficient for VARIANT.
- Regional context alone does not make two documents variants.
- A broad Hearth governance document and a regional cultural document are RELATED unless the pair is demonstrably two representations of the same governance subject/framework.
- Canonical umbrella ↔ regional canonical documents may be VARIANT when their subject is materially the same and the regional document is an explicit scoped expression of the umbrella subject.
- Comparative/reference documents should be RELATED to a source document unless they are explicitly representing the same underlying subject at a different scope/layer.

`REVIEW` remains reserved for genuinely ambiguous, conflicting, or insufficiently grounded cases.

This is a policy reference only. It does not alter canon or human labels.
